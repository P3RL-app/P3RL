P3RL – Social Stats

A fan-made phone app in the style of the Persona 3 Reload social stats screen. Do things in real life, log them, and watch your Charm, Courage and Academics rank up, with the Stats Up animation, the music notes and the rank-up sounds.

Open it here: https://p3rl-app.github.io/P3RL/

📱 This app is made for phones only

P3RL is built for a phone screen held upright. It opens on a computer, but the layout, the animations and the menu are made for a phone, so it won't look right there. Use it on your iPhone or Android phone.

🐣 How to install it (step by step, nothing to download)

You don't need the App Store or Google Play. It installs straight from the website.

iPhone
Open Safari. It has to be Safari, other browsers can't add it to the home screen.
Go to p3rl-app.github.io/P3RL
Tap the Share button at the bottom. It's the square with an arrow pointing up.
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
Tap Add in the top-right corner.
Close Safari. The P3RL icon is now on your home screen, so open it from there like any app.

Tip: sign in with Google once in Safari before adding it to your home screen. It goes smoother that way.

Android
Open Chrome.
Go to p3rl-app.github.io/P3RL
Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
Tap Add to Home screen (on some phones it says Install app).
Tap Add or Install.
Open P3RL from your home screen.

Android APK (optional)
Prefer an app file? Download P3RL.apk from this repository: https://github.com/P3RL-app/P3RL/raw/main/P3RL.apk
Open the file. If Android says it can't install unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
Tap Install, then Open.
The APK opens the same website inside an app window, so it always shows the newest version. Your phone may warn that the app is from an unknown developer. That's normal for apps from outside Google Play.
Tip: in the APK, sign in with email and password. Google sign-in may not open inside it.
🌐 Languages
P3RL comes in English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, Filipino, 日本語 and 한국어. It picks your phone's language by itself. To change it, open Menu → System → Config → Game System Settings → Language.

🖼️ Profile pictures
When you pick a profile picture, drag it to move and pinch or use the slider to zoom, then tap Use picture.

🎮 How to use it
New here? Menu → System → Tutorial explains everything step by step.
Log an activity: tap any activity in the list (search it, or filter by Charm, Courage or Academics). The Stats Up screen plays and your points go up. Tap OK to go back.
Your own activities: tap the NEW tab on the right edge of the screen. A drawer slides out where you write what happened, pick the stat and choose 1 to 3 points.
Rank up: enough points raises a stat to the next rank, up to rank 6, with the big rank-up animation.
Daily streak: every activity counts. Your first one each day adds +1 to your streak and the streak scene plays. Your streak is shown in the top-left of the stats screen. A grey flame means you haven't logged anything today yet.
Quests: Menu → Quests works like Elizabeth's requests. Tap Available to accept a quest and it shows In Progress. Only what you log after accepting counts. When it's done, tap Report for bonus points, and a harder quest of the same kind appears. Sort them by status with the Sort button.
Time of day: like the game, your day goes from Early Morning to Late Night. The date, time of day and moon show in the top-right of the stats screen, and the time change scene plays when it changes. Set your school days, class times, breaks and vacations in Menu → System → Config → Schedule Settings.
Undo: made a mistake? Tap Undo last on the main screen, or remove any entry in Menu → History.
Menu (☰ top right):
Announcements: news and updates about the app
Profile: your picture, banner, name, @username, description, ranks, and your Friends, Followers and Following
Social Link: your friends as Social Links, with their Arcana and rank, including friends who don't use the app
Messages: chat with your friends
Quests: this week's goals and your streak
History: everything you logged, day by day
Calendar: the game's calendar, where you can write your own plans on any day or repeat them every week, plus your Part-Time Jobs (pay, currency, day or night, work days, photo and color). Your work days show on the calendar.
System: the Tutorial and Config (sound, volumes, text speed, sizes, effects, your account and backups)
👥 Friends, Social Links and hangouts
Open Menu → System → Config → Account & Data and sign in with Google or with email and a password. A new email creates an account automatically.
Set up your profile: tap the circle to add a profile picture, tap the banner to add a banner, then pick a display name and an @username. If your name is taken, the app suggests free ones.
Pick your Arcana card in Menu → Profile.
Go to Menu → Social Link, search your friend's @username and tap Add. They accept it on their own phone, and the "I am thou" scene plays as your new Social Link begins.
Did something together? Tap the FRIEND tab on the left edge of the screen, pick the friend and what you did, and tap Send. Your friend gets a pop-up on their phone and taps Confirm. Then you both get the activity plus a +2 Charm bonus. Invites run out after 15 minutes. Every confirmed hangout grows your Social Link, up to MAX at Rank 10.
Friends not on P3RL: in Menu → Social Link, tap + New Social Link to add someone who doesn't use the app. Pick their name, picture and Arcana. Hangouts with them count right away, no confirming needed, and their Social Link ranks up just the same. Tap Edit on their Social Link to change or delete them.
💬 Messages
Menu → Messages: tap a friend to open your chat. You get a banner when someone texts you.
Tap any message to react, reply, edit your own message or copy it.
+ adds a picture. Inside the message box are GIFs (search, Trending, categories, Favorites and Recent), stickers you make yourself, and emojis, including Favorites (hold any emoji to add it) and your own custom emojis. When you make one you can share it to Community Emojis so everyone can use it. Custom emojis in your description show for everyone. Tap a friend's custom emoji to add it to your set.
💾 Your progress
Your progress is saved on your phone and stays after you close the app.
Sign in and it's also saved to your account. If you change phones or delete the app, just sign in again and everything comes back.
No account? Use Menu → System → Config → Account & Data → Copy backup to save it as text, and Paste backup to bring it back.

What friends can see: your picture, banner, name, @username, description and your three ranks. Your points and your history stay private.

❓ Something not working?
Menu or new features missing: fully close the app and open it again. If you added it to your home screen, close it from the app switcher. The newest version number is shown at the bottom of Config.
Sign-in doesn't open (iPhone): sign in once in Safari, then add the app to your home screen again.
No sound: check Config → Sound Settings, and make sure your phone isn't on silent.
